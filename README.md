# 🐰 BunnyIsland - Carrot Collection Adventure 🥕

A delightful browser-based game where you collect carrots, merge furniture, and build a bunny castle!

## 🎮 How to Play

### Getting Started
Simply open `index.html` in your web browser to start playing!

### Game Modes

#### 🥕 Carrot Collection (Button 1)
- **Click on carrots** to collect them
- Collect **10 carrots** to level up
- Special carrots:
  - ✨ **Golden Carrot**: Worth 3 regular carrots
  - 🌈 **Rainbow Carrot**: Worth 5 regular carrots
- Every **10 levels** unlocks a new themed bunny!

#### 🏰 Bunny Castle (Button 2)
- View all your unlocked bunnies playing in the castle
- Watch them hop around and interact
- Toggle between **day** ☀️ and **night** 🌙 modes
- Place furniture to make your castle cozy
- Bunnies will interact with furniture you place

#### ✨ Merge Furniture (Button 3)
- Click furniture at the bottom to add **Level 1** items to the grid
- Select an item, then click another to **merge** or **move**
- **Merging Rules**:
  - Must be the **same level** to merge
  - 2 Level 1 items → 1 Level 2 item
  - 2 Level 2 items → 1 Level 3 item, etc.
- **Thematic Merges**:
  - 💡 Lamp + 🗄️ Drawer = 💡🗄️ Lit Drawer
  - 💡 Lamp + 💡 Lamp = 💎💡 Chandelier
  - 🗄️ Drawer + 🗄️ Drawer = 🚪🗄️ Wardrobe
  - And many more!
